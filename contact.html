❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a plant?

Go to the Request a Plant page, fill out the form with your details and location, and submit. You'll receive a request ID to track progress.

How do I become a volunteer?

Visit the Volunteer page and register. Volunteers must be 14 or older, and those under 18 need parent/guardian consent.

How can I track my request?

Log in to your Dashboard to see all your requests and their current status.

How is plant health tracked?

After plantation, authorized volunteers conduct regular checks (1-month, 3-month). Updates are recorded on the Plant Care page.

Can I report an issue?

Yes! Use the contact form on this page or select "Report an Issue" as the subject.
