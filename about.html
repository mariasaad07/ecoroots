🌍 Our Story

ECO Roots started as a student project called "Green Islamabad" with a simple idea: make it easier for people to plant trees and track their growth.

Over time, it grew into a full platform connecting customers who want trees planted with volunteers and organizations who make it happen.

Today, we aim to cover all of Pakistan — from the streets of Islamabad to the mountains of the north and the plains of Punjab and Sindh.

Every plant counts. Every volunteer matters. Every area can be greener.
