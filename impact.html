🛤️ Our Journey

Milestones in our mission for a greener Pakistan

🌱

2026 — Launch

Platform launched with the first 100 plants planted in Islamabad & Rawalpindi. Building a foundation for community-driven greenery.

🌿

2027 — Expansion

Expanding to Lahore, Peshawar, and Quetta with a goal of 1,000+ plants. Growing our volunteer network across Pakistan.

🌳

2028 — National Coverage

Aiming for national coverage with 10,000 plants and 500+ active volunteers in every major city.

🌍

Future — Global Vision

Millions of plants, carbon offset tracking, corporate partnerships, and a sustainable green Pakistan for generations to come.
